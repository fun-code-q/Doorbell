Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This DPA forms part of the Terms of Service. It applies when QR Doorbell (the "Processor") processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller — typically a landlord, property management company, or coworking operator that deploys QR Doorbell across multiple units. It complies with Article 28 GDPR.

1. Roles & subject matter

Controller: the legal entity that uses QR Doorbell to receive doorbell rings on behalf of an organisation. Processor: the QR Doorbell maintainer (see CODEOWNERS). Processing covers the operation of the doorbell service described in the security model for the duration of the Controller's use of the service.

4. Security measures

The Processor implements the technical and organisational measures in the Security Model, including E2E encryption, RLS, HMAC-signed webhooks, rate limiting, audit-log redaction, retention windows, and the OSS-license screen for transparency.

5. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA (notably to Google for FCM delivery), the transfer is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC, 2021/914/EU) entered into with the relevant sub-processor.

6. Data subject rights

Visitors and owners may exercise their GDPR Articles 15–22 rights at any time:

  • Access & portability: Settings → About → Export my data (JSON).
  • Erasure: Settings → About → Delete account. Cascades server-side; nothing of yours remains.
  • Rectification: edit your profile in Settings.
  • Restriction / objection: via the maintainer contact below.

8. Data Protection Officer

Until the Processor reaches the GDPR Art. 37 threshold, a DPO is not strictly required. For privacy enquiries, contact the maintainer listed in CODEOWNERS. For production B2B deployments processing data from more than 1,000 visitors per month, a named DPO should be appointed and listed here.

9. Breach notification

The Processor will notify the Controller of any Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, including a description of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, and the measures taken or proposed.
